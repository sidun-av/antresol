Your media library. On all your devices.

Antresol is a self-hosted home media server and a player app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The films, TV shows, music, books, audiobooks and comics you keep at home, in one library designed in the Apple style.

Install the serverConnect the app

Films and TV shows

Pick up where you left off, Picture in Picture, AirPlay, subtitles and audio tracks.

Music

Albums, artists, playlists, favourites and lyrics. Background playback.

Books and comics

EPUB, FB2, PDF, CBZ: a reader with themes, contents and bookmarks.

Audiobooks

Chapters, speed, sleep timer; your position syncs across devices.

Offline

Save a film, an album or a book to your device before a trip.

Family

Profiles with passwords and kid profiles with age limits.

How it works

The Antresol server runs on your own computer or NAS and shows the files you put there. The app connects only to your server. The developer provides and sells no content and receives none of your data.

Only add media you have the rights to.
