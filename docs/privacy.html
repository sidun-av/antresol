Antresol Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026.

Antresol is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that browses and plays your own media library: films, TV shows, music, books, comics and audiobooks. The library lives on an Antresol server that you, or your family's administrator, run on your own computer or NAS. The developer provides no content and runs no servers that your data passes through.

Summary

  • The developer collects no data. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs or crash reporting of its own.
  • The app exchanges data only with the server address you enter. It also loads some artwork from public websites (see below).
  • Your sign-in token is stored in the Keychain on your device.
  • Files you save for offline use stay on your device and are removed with the app.

What is processed, and where

On your Antresol server

Your server is not the developer's server. Its administrator, usually you, controls it. The app sends it:

  • your profile name and password when you sign in. The password is checked by the server and is not stored by the app;
  • a device name, shown in the server's list of devices. On iPhone and iPad this is the device type (for example "iPhone"). On a Mac it is the computer's name, which often contains your own name. The server's administrator can remove a device from that list;
  • the device model (for example "iPhone17,3"), the app version and a random identifier of this installation, so that the server can tell your devices apart;
  • the interface language of the app, so that the server answers in that language;
  • what you search for in your library;
  • your viewing, reading and listening positions, "Watched" marks, your Watchlist, favourites and playlists;
  • requests for copies that you download to the device.

Song lyrics, subtitles and descriptions are fetched by your server, not by the app. The app gets them only from your server.

All of this is stored on your server and is governed by its administrator's rules. The developer has no access to it.

On your device

  • A sign-in token for each profile, in the device Keychain. Only this app can read it, and it is not synced to your other devices.
  • The list of servers and profiles, your settings (reading theme, video quality, "Download over Wi-Fi Only" and so on) and a random device identifier, in the app's settings.
  • Files you download to the device, cached artwork and books, and playback positions not yet sent to the server, in the app's container. The app checks free storage so that it does not fill up your device, and shows it on the Downloads screen.

These are removed when you delete the app. On iPhone and iPad, Keychain entries may survive the deletion; they are useless without the server and are overwritten the next time you sign in.

Artwork from public websites

Your server points to some posters, backdrops and covers on public websites: The Movie Database (TMDB), Cover Art Archive and Open Library. The app loads these images directly, without any of your sign-in data. Those sites see the device's IP address and the image requested, as with any web page, and their own privacy policies apply.

Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics about the app with the developer. This is controlled by Apple and by your settings, not by the app.

Network and security

  • The app connects to the server address you entered, at home or over the internet.
  • At home, iOS and macOS may ask for permission to access the local network. The app uses it only to reach your server.
  • If an address outside your home network starts with http:// rather than https://, sign-in data travels unencrypted. The app warns you about this. For access over the internet, use https or a VPN such as Tailscale.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them. Kid profiles and age limits are set up by your server's administrator.

Demo server for App Review

The developer may run a demonstration server with openly licensed content so that Apple can review the app. Its logs contain only the reviewers' actions and are used only to run that server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/sidun-av/antresol/issues.
